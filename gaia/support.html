Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device, and also in your own iCloud account if you are signed in to iCloud — so an iPhone and an iPad on the same Apple ID share one save. If both devices were played separately, the game asks which progress to keep.

Is the game free?
Chapter 1 (the Hive) is free up to difficulty D2. One purchase unlocks the full game: Chapters 2–4 (the Rotmire, the Rustworks and the Dragon Realm), difficulty D3 and up with no limit, and rebirth. It is a one-time purchase that works on every device with your Apple ID; use Settings → Restore purchases on a new device.

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. GAIA plays fully offline.

Does anything happen while I'm away?
Yes. Your camp keeps producing cores while the game is closed, for up to 8 hours.

How do I change the music or sound volume?
Home → Settings. You can set music and sound effects separately, or mute both.

Which languages does the game support?
English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Polish and Russian. The game follows your device language; to change it, go to Home → Settings → Language.

How do I see the tutorial again?
Home → Settings → Replay the tutorial. It replays the guided first battle and the one-time tips on each screen.

常見問題

我的進度存在哪裡？
存在你的裝置上；如果登入了 iCloud，也會存一份在你自己的 iCloud 帳號裡，同一個 Apple 帳號的 iPhone 和 iPad 共用同一份進度。兩台各自玩過的話，遊戲會問你要留哪一份。

遊戲是免費的嗎？
第 1 章「蟲巢」的難度階 D0–D2 免費。一次購買解鎖完整遊戲：第 2–4 章「腐沼」「鏽械」「龍域」、難度階 D3 以上（沒有上限）、轉生。一次買斷、同一個 Apple 帳號的每台裝置都能用；換新裝置時到 設定 → 恢復購買。

需要連網嗎？
不需要，GAIA 可以完全離線遊玩。

離開遊戲的時候會發生什麼事嗎？
會。關掉遊戲的時候，營地會繼續累積核心，最多 8 小時。

怎麼調音樂和音效的音量？
首頁 → 設定。音樂和音效可以分開調，也可以一起靜音。

支援哪些語言？
繁體中文、简体中文、English、日本語、한국어、Deutsch、Français、Español、Italiano、Português (Brasil)、Polski、Русский。遊戲照裝置的語言顯示；要換的話到 首頁 → 設定 → 語言。

怎麼重看新手教學？
首頁 → 設定 → 重看新手教學。會重新打一次教學戰鬥，每個畫面的說明也會再出現一次。
