Respite — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Respite is a single-player game. We designed it to work without collecting anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Respite does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.
  • The game itself does not connect to our servers or to any third party. Buying or restoring the full game is handled entirely by Apple's App Store, and syncing your progress by your own iCloud account; we do not receive your payment details, account details or game data.

Data stored on your device and in your iCloud

Your game progress and settings are saved on your device, inside the app's own storage. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of your progress is also kept in your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage) so it can sync between your iPhone and iPad. It goes only to your iCloud account, under Apple's own privacy terms; we never receive it. If you use iCloud or computer backups, Apple's backup service may also include this data in your normal device backup.

Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. The small copy in your iCloud stays with your iCloud account and is used again if you install the game on another device or reinstall it.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: flexiblepackage.support+respite@gmail.com

Respite 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 23 日

Respite 是一款單人遊戲，設計上不需要蒐集任何關於你的資訊。

我們蒐集什麼

什麼都不蒐集。Respite 不蒐集、不儲存、不傳送、不出售、也不分享任何個人資料或使用資料。

  • 不需要帳號，也沒有登入。
  • 沒有廣告，不使用廣告識別碼。
  • 沒有任何分析、當機回報或追蹤。
  • 遊戲本身不連到我們的伺服器或任何第三方。購買或恢復完整版全部由 Apple 的 App Store 處理，進度同步走你自己的 iCloud 帳號；我們拿不到你的付款、帳號或遊戲資料。

存在你裝置上與你 iCloud 裡的資料

你的遊戲進度與設定存在你的裝置上、這個 app 自己的儲存空間裡。如果你登入了 iCloud，進度也會在你自己的 iCloud 帳號裡留一份（Apple 的 iCloud 鍵值儲存），讓 iPhone 和 iPad 之間同步；這份資料只會到你自己的 iCloud 帳號、適用 Apple 自己的隱私條款，我們收不到。如果你使用 iCloud 或電腦備份，Apple 的備份服務也可能在你平常的裝置備份中包含這些資料。

刪除 app 會刪除那台裝置上的資料。你 iCloud 裡那一小份會留在你的 iCloud 帳號，在別台裝置安裝或重新安裝時會再用到。

兒童

遊戲不向任何人蒐集資料，所以也不向兒童蒐集資料。

變更

這份政策如有變更，新版本會公布在這個頁面，並標上新的生效日期。
