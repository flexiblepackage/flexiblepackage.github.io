Contact

For questions, bug reports, or feedback, email flexiblepackage.support+respite@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, and the game version (shown at the bottom right of the main menu).

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account so your progress syncs between your iPhone and iPad. There are no accounts, and we never receive your saves.

How do I change the language?
Main menu → Settings → Language.

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Respite plays fully offline. Buying or restoring the full game needs a connection to the App Store, and iCloud sync happens when you are online.

Is the game free?
You can play as many expeditions as you like for free, with the Arrow and Fire Towers and the inner part of the skill tree (each upgrade up to level 2). A one-time purchase opens the whole skill tree - the Pulse and Lightning Towers, four terrain islands, Mastery and every upgrade at full level - no subscriptions, no ads.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Main menu → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.
