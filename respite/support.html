Contact

For questions, bug reports, or feedback, email flexiblepackage.support+respite@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, and the game version (shown at the bottom right of the main menu).

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. The game does not use accounts or online saves, so progress does not move between devices.

How do I change the language?
Main menu → Settings → Language.

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Respite plays fully offline. Only buying or restoring the full game needs a connection to the App Store.

Is the game free?
You can play as many expeditions as you like for free, with the Arrow and Fire Towers and most of the skill tree. A one-time purchase unlocks the Pulse and Lightning Towers, four terrain islands and Mastery - no subscriptions, no ads.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Main menu → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

常見問題

我的進度存在哪裡？
只存在你的裝置上。遊戲沒有帳號，也沒有雲端存檔，所以進度不會在不同裝置之間同步。

怎麼換語言？
主選單 → 設定 → 語言。

需要連網嗎？
不需要，Respite 可以完全離線遊玩。只有購買或恢復完整版時要連上 App Store。

遊戲免費嗎？
免費就能玩，遠征不限次數：箭塔、火焰塔與大部分的技能樹。一次購買解鎖脈衝塔、雷電塔、四座地形島與精通，不用訂閱、沒有廣告。

換新手機怎麼恢復購買？
主選單 → 設定 → 恢復購買（要登入當初購買的同一個 Apple 帳號）。
